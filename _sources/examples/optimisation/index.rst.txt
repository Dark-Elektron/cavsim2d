Optimisation
============

Multi-objective evolutionary shape optimisation over a cavity's design
variables. A minimal two-objective run comes first, then the tools for reading
a finished search, then optimisation of non-elliptical cavities. The last two
examples treat the whole multi-cell cavity: first why keeping it on frequency
needs a second constraint (field flatness), then a whole-cavity optimisation
that relies on it.
