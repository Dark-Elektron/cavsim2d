Tuning
======

Adjusting a cavity's geometry so it meets a target. The examples run from a
single cell with one variable tuned to one frequency, through reading a tune
that cannot converge, to a multicell cavity, the design family a two-cell tune
opens up, and finally tuning to a figure of merit alongside the frequency ---
several targets, and a matching number of variables.
