Wakefield
=========

Beam-induced wake potentials and the impedance spectrum, via the ABCI backend.
One cavity first, then the same run on other geometries, then a parameter sweep.
The section closes with the eigenmode route to the same impedance, reconstructed
from open-boundary modes and compared against the wakefield. The multicell
wakefield under manufacturing uncertainty is in :doc:`../advanced/index`.
